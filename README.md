> A curated list of tools, frameworks, methodologies, platforms, books, communities, and resources for Product Management.

Product Management sits at the intersection of technology, business, design, and customer needs. Effective product managers help teams identify opportunities, prioritize work, define strategy, validate assumptions, and deliver products that create value for users and organizations.

This list is intended for product managers, product owners, founders, entrepreneurs, UX professionals, business analysts, software teams, and anyone involved in building and managing products.

## Product Strategy

Frameworks, methodologies, and resources for defining product vision, goals, and direction.

- [Lean Startup](https://theleanstartup.com/) — Methodology focused on validated learning and iterative product development.
- [Business Model Canvas](https://www.strategyzer.com/canvas/business-model-canvas) — Strategic framework for designing business models.
- [Value Proposition Canvas](https://www.strategyzer.com/canvas/value-proposition-canvas) — Framework for understanding customer needs and product value.
- [OKRs](https://www.whatmatters.com/) — Goal-setting framework used by product organizations.
- [North Star Framework](https://amplitude.com/north-star) — Product strategy framework centered around a key success metric.
- [Product Vision Board](https://www.romanpichler.com/tools/product-vision-board/) — Tool for defining product vision and direction.

## Roadmapping & Prioritization

Tools and frameworks for planning, prioritizing, and communicating product initiatives.

- [Aha!](https://www.aha.io/) — Product roadmapping and strategy platform.
- [Productboard](https://www.productboard.com/) — Product management and prioritization platform.
- [Roadmunk](https://roadmunk.com/) — Roadmapping and product planning software.
- [Jira Product Discovery](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/product-discovery) — Product prioritization and discovery platform.
- [RICE Framework](https://www.intercom.com/resources/books/intercom-product-management/roadmap-and-prioritization/prioritizing-your-roadmap) — Prioritization method based on Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort.
- [MoSCoW Method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MoSCoW_method) — Prioritization framework for product requirements.
